# Bloom Template – Wedding Invitation Website (Exact Replica)

Ye website **Bloom Template** (`https://bloom-template.thedigitalyes.com/`) ka **exact 100% clone/replica** hai. Saare elements, designs, animations, high-resolution illustrations, photos, audio music, intro videos, RSVP form aur admin dashboard replicate kiye gaye hain.

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

1. **Cinematic Opening Screen ("Tap to open")**:
   - Interactive opening experience with wax seal envelope animation.
   - Dual high-definition intro video sequence (`intro-video-new-bkm1XghM.mp4` & `intro-video-2-a-H3NxSy.mp4`).

2. **Romantic Background Music Player**:
   - Floating audio widget with sound wave animation icon.
   - Background track: `wedding-background-music-yxy0nS2O.mp3`.
   - Seamless Play / Pause / Mute control.

3. **Hero Section**:
   - Curved SVG typography arch ("WE'RE GETTING MARRIED").
   - Shubham & Vrinda couple header with floral border illustrations.

4. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Real-time countdown to 25th & 26th November 2026 (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds).

5. **Welcome & Family Section**:
   - "Together with their families" botanical layout featuring Shubham & Vrinda.

6. **Ceremony & Reception Details**:
   - Radisson Blu, Nathdwara, Rajasthan.
   - "Open in Maps" (Google Maps & Apple Maps direct links).
   - "Add to Calendar" (.ics calendar download + Google Calendar link).

7. **Wedding Day Programme & Celebration Timeline**:
   - **Programme Frame (1st Block)**: Wednesday, November 25 Morning Pooja (Breakfast Blu Pavillion, Vinayak Sthapna, Mangal Kalash) in floral wavy frame.
   - **Celebration Timeline (2nd Block)**: Complete vertical dotted timeline with custom watercolor icons for Carnival Bloom & Haldi, Sangeet – Written in the Stars, Mayra & Sajjangot, and Sacred Vows (Baarat Swagat, Safa Bandhi, Toran, Pheras).
